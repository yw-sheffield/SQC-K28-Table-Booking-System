Privacy notice — how your booking data is handled

What we collect. When you book a desk we store the desk, the date, the time slot, and the name you type in. We ask for a name only so colleagues can see who has a desk; a first name or initials is enough. Please don't enter anything you wouldn't want others who use this booking page to see.

Why. The only purpose is managing shared desks. Bookings are visible to anyone with access to this page.

How long. Bookings are automatically removed 30 days after their date. You can cancel any booking you made from this device at any time.

Where. In demo mode, data stays only in this browser and is never sent anywhere.

Your rights. Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of, correction of, or deletion of your data. Update the contact details in this notice before going live.
